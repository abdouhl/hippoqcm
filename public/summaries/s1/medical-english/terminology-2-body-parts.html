If you remember only this
  • Brain = control centre · heart = pump · lungs = O₂ in, CO₂ out · liver = processes nutrients, detoxifies, makes bile · kidneys = filter waste, fluid balance.
  • The skin is the largest organ (barrier against injury, germs, temperature changes).
  • Immune system defends against infection; endocrine system releases hormones (growth, metabolism, mood).
  • Body-part roots: cardio (heart), derma (skin), neuro (nerve).
  • Grammar: has with a singular subject (The patient has pain), are with a plural one (The kidneys are…); questions with Does … have / Are ….

2. Systems and senses p. 3

  • Circulatory system works with the respiratory and digestive systems to deliver nutrients and oxygen.
  • Immune system: defence against infections. Endocrine system: hormones regulating growth, metabolism and mood.
  • Five senses and organs: sight (eyes), hearing (ears), smell (nose), taste (tongue), touch (skin).
  • Knowing how the body works helps everyone make informed choices about nutrition, exercise and medical care. (Source: Anatomy and Physiology: The Unity of Form and Function.)

3. Grammar: have/has and is/are p. 4

StatementQuestion
The patient has pain in the chest.Does the patient have pain in the chest?
The liver has important functions.Does the liver have important functions?
The kidneys are responsible for filtering blood.Are the kidneys responsible for filtering blood?
The eyes are sensitive to light.Are the eyes sensitive to light?
Trap After does, use the base form have — never "Does the liver has…?". With be, just invert: "Are the eyes…?"

3–4. Describing complaints ("Doctor! I need your help!") p. 4–5

"My head hurts" · "My nose is runny" · "My eyes are dry and watery" · "My throat is dry" · "My chest feels tight — I can't breathe" · "My stomach hurts" · "My skin is itchy" · "I have a toothache" · "My knees keep locking" · "My legs feel weak" · "I twisted my ankle".

Role-play: Doctor: Where do you feel pain? — Patient: I feel pain in my chest, near the heart. — Doctor: Do you have problems breathing with your lungs? — Patient: Yes, especially when I walk.

Self-check — answer, then tap to reveal

Which organ is the largest, and what does it protect against?
The skin — injury, germs, temperature changes.
Three functions of the liver?
Processes nutrients, detoxifies harmful substances, produces bile.
What do the kidneys do?
Filter waste from the blood and maintain fluid balance.
Which system releases hormones, and what do they regulate?
The endocrine system — growth, metabolism, mood.
Turn into a question: "The heart has four chambers."
Does the heart have four chambers?

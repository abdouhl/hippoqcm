If you remember only this
  • The cardiovascular (= circulatory) system = heart + blood vessels + blood: delivers oxygen, nutrients, hormones; removes CO₂ and wastes.
  • Heart: 4 chambers — 2 atria (top), 2 ventricles (bottom). Right side → lungs; left side → systemic circulation. Separation prevents O₂-rich and O₂-poor blood from mixing.
  • Arteries carry blood away under strong pressure · veins return it with valves · capillaries = exchange sites.
  • Blood = connective tissue: plasma, red cells (hemoglobin → O₂), white cells (defence), platelets (clotting).
  • Regulation: autonomic nervous system (heart rate, vessel diameter) + hormones (adrenaline). Grammar focus: the passive voice.

2. The heart p. 3

  • The engine of the system; a muscular organ with four chambers: two atria at the top, two ventricles at the bottom.
  • Right side: pumps blood to the lungs (O₂ absorbed, CO₂ released). Left side: propels oxygenated blood into the systemic circulation, to every organ.
  • The strict right/left division ensures efficiency by preventing mixing of oxygen-rich and oxygen-poor blood.

2. Blood vessels p. 3

VesselRole
ArteriesCarry blood away from the heart under strong pressure
VeinsReturn blood to the heart with the help of valves
CapillariesSmallest vessels; fine exchange sites (O₂ and nutrients in, wastes out) — fundamental for cellular survival

2. Blood and regulation p. 3

ComponentRole
PlasmaLiquid part of blood
Red blood cellsContain hemoglobin, transport oxygen
White blood cellsDefend the body against pathogens
PlateletsClot formation → protect against excessive bleeding

Blood is a specialized connective tissue. Neural and hormonal control: the autonomic nervous system adjusts heart rate and vessel diameter; adrenaline prepares the system for stress or effort. The system also takes part in temperature regulation and immune defence. (Source: Principles of Anatomy and Physiology.)

3. Grammar: the passive voice p. 4–5

Passive = object becomes subject + be (in the right tense) + past participle (+ by agent).

ActivePassive
The heart pumps blood to the lungs.Blood is pumped to the lungs by the heart.
Red blood cells transport oxygen.Oxygen is transported by red blood cells.
The autonomic nervous system adjusts the heart rate.The heart rate is adjusted by the autonomic nervous system.
White blood cells defend the body against pathogens.The body is defended against pathogens by white blood cells.

Model: "Oxygen is absorbed in the lungs and is then transported to tissues by red blood cells."

If you remember only this
  • Medical terms: mostly Greek and Latin, some from the time of Hippocrates (1st century B.C.); still growing (MRI, ultrasound).
  • 3 parts: root (core meaning — every term has at least one), prefix (beginning), suffix (end). Not all terms have a prefix.
  • Combining form = root + vowel (usually o). Root + root → keep o; root + suffix starting with a consonant → keep o (cardiology); suffix starting with a vowel → drop o (hepatitis).
  • Exception: second root starting with a vowel can drop the o: lymphadenopathy.
  • Plurals: -a→-ae · -ma→-mata · -us→-i · -is→-es · -nx→-nges · -ex/-ix→-ices · -on→-a.

1–2.1 Medical terms and roots p. 2–5

  • Traditional terms: carditis (inflammation of the heart), osteotomy (cutting of bone); modern: MRI, ultrasound (US).
  • Everyday analogy, root act (to do): react, enact, transact, exact, actor, active, action — change any part, the meaning changes.
  • Root = stem, usually Greek/Latin, often a body part, condition or procedure. Tree image: root = trunk; prefix = root system; suffix = branches and leaves.
  • arthr (joint): poly + arthr + itis = polyarthritis (inflammation of many joints) · arthroscopy (look inside a joint) · arthroplasty (surgical repair of a joint).
  • Roots can sit at the beginning (hepatitis), middle (encephalitis) or end (psychopath).

Ruleo?Example
1. Root + rootYes (almost always)cardiomyopathy — disease of the heart muscle
2a. Root + suffix starting with a consonant (-logy, -gram, -pathy)Yescardiology

Self-check — answer, then tap to reveal

Keep or drop the o: gastr + -scopy? hepat + -itis? cardi + my + pathy?
gastroscopy (keep) · hepatitis (drop) · cardiomyopathy (keep between roots).
Why is it lymphadenopathy and not lymphoadenopathy?
The second root (aden) starts with a vowel, so the o is dropped to make it easier to say.
Give the meaning of: arthroplasty, hepatomegaly, cardialgia, endocardium.
Surgical repair of a joint · enlargement of the liver · heart pain · inner lining of the heart.
Where is the root in hepatitis, encephalitis, psychopath?
Beginning · middle · end.
Plural of: adenoma, phalanx, ganglion, testis, villus.
adenomata · phalanges · ganglia · testes · villi.
Which part of "subhepatic" means "below"?
sub-.

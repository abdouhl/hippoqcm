If you remember only this
  • Clavicle, inferior surface: costal tuberosity medially (costoclavicular lig.), subclavius groove in the middle, trapezoid line + conoid tubercle laterally. Anterior border: concave lateral 1/3, convex medial 2/3.
  • Scapula: projects from the 2nd to the 7th rib. Borders: superior (cervical) with the scapular notch, medial (spinal), lateral (axillary). Lateral angle = coracoid process + glenoid cavity + neck (which carries the glenoid).
  • Glenoid neck tubercles: supraglenoid → biceps (long head), infraglenoid → triceps (long head). Coracoid → biceps short head, coracobrachialis, pectoralis minor.
  • Humerus: diaphysis projections = crest of the lesser tubercle (latissimus dorsi), deltoid tuberosity, crest of the greater tubercle (pectoralis major). Radial nerve groove on the posterior surface. Condyle: capitulum lateral (→ radial head), trochlea medial (→ ulnar trochlear notch).
  • Ulna (medial): olecranon vertical & posterior, coronoid horizontal & anterior; coronoid carries the lower trochlear notch, the radial notch (laterally) and the ulnar tuberosity (brachialis).
  • Radius (lateral): head = fovea on top (→ capitulum) + articular circumference around (→ ulna radial notch, annular lig.); radial tuberosity → biceps brachii; medial border = interosseous.
  • Carpus lateral → medial: proximal row scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum, pisiform; distal row trapezium, trapezoid, capitate, hamate.

II. Clavicle p. 1–2 2023-24 · 2025-26

Long bone shaped like a stretched "S", between the scapula and the sternum. Set up: flattened end lateral; the concave border of that end forward; the surface with a groove downward.

III. Scapula p. 2 2023-24 · 2024-25 · 2025-26

Flat, triangular, dorsal bone lying against the rib cage; projects onto the 2nd rib above and the 7th rib below; articulates with the humerus and clavicle. Set up: concave face forward; oval articular surface lateral; notched border upward.

PartFeatures
Anterior (costal) surfaceExcavated = subscapular fossa, crossed by 2–3 oblique ridges → subscapularis
Posterior (dorsal) surfaceDivided by the scapular spine into supraspinous fossa (→ supraspinatus) and infraspinous fossa (→ infraspinatus, teres minor, teres major).
Spine = triangular bony plate, perpendicular to the posterior surface, extended laterally by the acromion (clavicular articular surface → clavicle).
Superior (cervical) border 🎯Scapular notch, bridged by the transverse scapular ligament; passage of the suprascapular artery and nerve

Lateral (axillary) border 🎯—
Superior angle—
Inferior angle= tip of the scapula
Lateral angle 🎯3 structures: coracoid process, glenoid cavity, neck

Lateral angle in detail 🎯

IV. Humerus p. 3–4 2023-24 · 2025-26

Long bone forming alone the arm skeleton; articulates with the scapula above and the forearm bones below. Set up: rounded end up; its articular surface medial; the groove running down that end forward.

Diaphysis — cylindrical above, triangular and flattened below; 3 surfaces, 3 borders 🎯

PartFeatures
Anteromedial surfaceCrest of the lesser tubercle → latissimus dorsi
Anterolateral surfaceDeltoid tuberosity (deltoid "V") → deltoid
Posterior surfaceCrossed by the radial nerve groove (radial nerve; lesion shown on slide p. 58). Triceps: lateral head above the groove, medial head below
Anterior border= crest of the greater tubercle → pectoralis major
Medial and lateral borders—

Proximal epiphysis — 6 structures

StructureFeatures
Humeral head1/3 of a sphere, radius 30 mm; articulates with the glenoid cavity
Greater tubercleLarge lateral projection
Lesser tubercleSmall anterior projection
Intertubercular grooveBetween the 2 tubercles; covered by the transverse humeral ligament; passage of the biceps long-head tendon
Anatomical neckSeparates the head from the 2 tubercles
Surgical neckSeparates the proximal epiphysis from the diaphysis; vulnerable to fractures

Distal epiphysis — flattened, 3 structures 🎯

StructureFeatures
Humeral condyle (articular)3 surfaces:
• Capitulum: rounded lateral projection, not visible from behind, topped by the radial fossa; → radial head
• Trochlea: medial pulley, 2 cheeks separated by a groove; topped by the coronoid fossa (front) and olecranon fossa (back); → trochlear notch of the ulna
• Capitulo-trochlear (conoid) zone, between them
Medial epicondyleVery developed, palpable; ulnar collateral ligament of the elbow; crossed behind by the ulnar nerve groove
Lateral epicondylePoorly developed; radial collateral ligament of the elbow

V.1 Ulna p. 4 2023-24 · 2024-25 · 2025-26

The two forearm bones are joined by the proximal and distal radio-ulnar joints and the antebrachial interosseous membrane.

Medial forearm bone; articulates with the humeral trochlea above, the radius laterally, the radio-ulnar articular disc below. Set up: largest end up; its hook-shaped articular surface forward; the small facet of that end lateral.

PartFeatures
Diaphysis3 surfaces: anterior, medial, posterior. 3 borders: anterior, posterior, lateral = interosseous (interosseous membrane)
Proximal epiphysis2 processes forming a "hook" that carries the trochlear notch (→ humeral trochlea)
• Olecranon 🎯Vertical and posterior, subcutaneous; carries the upper part of the trochlear notch; insertion of the triceps brachii
• Coronoid process 🎯Horizontal and anterior. Above: lower part of the trochlear notch. Laterally: radial notch (→ radial articular circumference). In front: ulnar tuberosity → brachialis
Distal epiphysisUlnar head → ulnar notch of the radius + radio-ulnar articular disc of the wrist.
Ulnar styloid process: small posteromedial projection → radio-ulnar disc + ulnar collateral ligament of the carpus

V.2 Radius p. 4–5 2023-24 · 2024-25 · 2025-26

Lateral forearm bone; articulates with the capitulum above, the ulna medially, the carpus below. Set up: sharpest border medial; largest end down; the grooved face of that end backward.

PartFeatures
Diaphysis3 surfaces: anterior, lateral, posterior. 3 borders: anterior, posterior, medial = interosseous 🎯 (interosseous membrane; faces the ulna's lateral border)
Proximal epiphysis (cylindrical)Radial head 🎯:
• Articular fovea (cupula): superior, concave → humeral capitulum and capitulo-trochlear zone
• Articular circumference: peripheral (the rim) → radial notch of the ulna and the annular ligament
Radial neck.
Radial tuberosity: ovoid projection where the biceps brachii ends
Distal epiphysisQuadrangular pyramid with an inferior base:
• Lateral face → radial styloid process, bounded by 2 grooves for 3 tendons
• Posterior face → hollowed by 2 grooves for 7 tendons
• Medial face → ulnar notch (→ ulnar head)
• Base → carpal articular surface (→ the carpus)

Memory trick (mnemonic, not from the course) Lateral → medial, proximal then distal: She Looks Too Pretty; Try To Catch Her = Scaphoid, Lunate, Triquetrum, Pisiform, Trapezium, Trapezoid, Capitate, Hamate.

Three remarks p. 5

  1. Sesamoid bones: small bones on the palmar surface of the digital joints.
  2. Bone development: membranous or cartilaginous ossification. After birth: growth in width at the periosteum, in length at the growth (conjugation) plate.
  3. Bone age (children, adolescents): assessed on an X-ray of the left wrist and hand, from the ossification of the carpal bones and of the epiphyses of the long bones of wrist and hand.

Exam traps — the wrong options past exams used

False statements to recognise
  • Clavicle: "conoid tubercle on the ventral surface", "costoclavicular ligament on the cranial surface", "anterior border concave medially", "acromial surface on the inferior surface" — false. 2 years
  • Scapula borders "spinal superior", "spinal lateral", "medial = thickest" — false: superior = cervical, lateral = axillary, medial = spinal. 2024-25
  • Scapula "tip opposite the 8th rib" — false per the course (2nd–7th ribs). "The cervical border shows the coracoid notch" — false (it shows the scapular notch). 2023-24
  • Lateral angle includes the "pillar" or the "coracoid/scapular notch"; "the neck supports the coracoid"; "supraglenoid → triceps" — false: neck supports the glenoid; supraglenoid → biceps. 2 years
  • Humerus: "proximal epiphysis entirely articular", "distal epiphysis articulates only with the ulna" — false. "Radial nerve groove" and "medial epicondyle" are not diaphyseal projections. 2 years
  • Radius: "fovea articulates with the medial part of the condyle / with the ulna", "articular circumference = fovea / articulates with the capitulum", "styloid on the medial surface", "proximal epiphysis has a single articular surface" — false. 2 years
  • Distal radius: "ventral surface hollowed by grooves" (it's the dorsal one); "inferior surface carries the ulnar notch" (it's the medial face) — false. 2024-25
  • Radius medial border "separates anterior and lateral surfaces", "continues on both epiphyses" — false. 2025-26
  • Ulna: "olecranon horizontal and anterior", "coronoid vertical and posterior", "radial notch on the medial surface", "ulnar head has a single articular surface"; coronoid carries the "upper trochlear notch" or "ulnar notch / styloid" — false. 3 years
  • Carpus: "scaphoid is metacarpal / distal row / medial", "trapezoid in the proximal row", "capitate in the middle of the proximal row" — false. 2 years
Where the official keys go beyond the PDF Some keyed answers aren't written in this PDF: 2024-25 Q31 counts the distal radius's ventral surface as smooth and its inferior surface as triangular; 2023-24 Q22 keys "the trapezoid ligament attaches to the ventral tubercle of the caudal surface" as true. Learn them as the keys give them.

Self-check — answer, then tap to reveal

Describe the 3 zones of the clavicle's inferior surface.
Lateral: trapezoid line + conoid tubercle (trapezoid and conoid ligaments). Middle: subclavius groove. Medial: costal tuberosity (costoclavicular ligament).
What does each end of the clavicle articulate with?
Acromial end (lateral, flattened) → acromion. Sternal end (medial, rounded) → sternum and 1st costal cartilage.
Between which ribs does the scapula project? Name its borders.
2nd to 7th rib. Superior (cervical), medial (spinal), lateral (axillary).
What passes through the scapular notch?
The suprascapular artery and nerve (under the transverse scapular ligament).
Which muscles insert on the coracoid process? On the supra- and infraglenoid tubercles?
Coracoid: biceps short head, coracobrachialis, pectoralis minor. Supraglenoid: biceps long head. Infraglenoid: triceps long head.
Which muscles occupy the scapular fossae?
Subscapular: subscapularis. Supraspinous: supraspinatus. Infraspinous: infraspinatus, teres minor, teres major.
List the projections of the humeral diaphysis and their muscles.
Crest of the lesser tubercle (latissimus dorsi), deltoid tuberosity (deltoid), crest of the greater tubercle = anterior border (pectoralis major).
Anatomical vs surgical neck of the humerus?
Anatomical: between head and tubercles. Surgical: between proximal epiphysis and diaphysis, prone to fractures.
What lies in the intertubercular groove?
The long-head tendon of biceps brachii, under the transverse humeral ligament.
Capitulum vs trochlea: position, fossa above, partner.
Capitulum: lateral, radial fossa, radial head. Trochlea: medial, coronoid fossa (front) and olecranon fossa (back), ulnar trochlear notch.
Olecranon vs coronoid process.
Olecranon: vertical, posterior, subcutaneous, upper trochlear notch, triceps. Coronoid: horizontal, anterior, lower trochlear notch, radial notch (lateral), ulnar tuberosity (brachialis).
The 2 articular parts of the radial head and their partners.
Fovea (superior, concave) → capitulum and capitulo-trochlear zone. Articular circumference (peripheral) → radial notch of the ulna and annular ligament.
Describe the 4 faces of the distal radius.
Lateral: radial styloid, 2 grooves, 3 tendons. Posterior: 2 grooves, 7 tendons. Medial: ulnar notch. Base: carpal articular surface.
Name the carpal bones row by row, lateral to medial.
Proximal: scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum, pisiform. Distal: trapezium, trapezoid, capitate, hamate.
How many phalanges, and what passes through the carpal tunnel?
14 (3 per finger, 2 for the thumb). Median nerve and muscle tendons.
